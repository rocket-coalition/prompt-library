Chain of Thought

RSN-001 · Reasoning Pattern

Intention

Forces step-by-step reasoning before reaching conclusions. Dramatically improves accuracy on complex problems by making the reasoning process explicit.

Pattern Template
# Chain of Thought Pattern reasoning: approach: "step-by-step" instruction: | Think through this problem step by step. Show your reasoning at each stage. Only provide your final answer after working through all steps. format: - "Step 1: [identify the core problem]" - "Step 2: [break into components]" - "Step 3: [solve each part]" - "Step 4: [synthesize conclusion]" - "Final Answer: [clear statement]"
When to Use

Math problems, logical puzzles, complex analysis, multi-step reasoning tasks, any situation where showing work improves accuracy.
