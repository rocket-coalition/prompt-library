### The Seven Principles of The Magical Recipe

These principles detail how the four components of the DMAP pipeline are constructed to execute the laws and guarantee the desired outcome.

#### 1. The Seed Input (User's Thought)
* **Principle of Codified Intent:** The user's input must be immediately and automatically parsed into the **Intention Packet** (the four-liner of Rich desire, Will, and Intention) to activate the process. *A query is never just a query; it is an ignition command.*

#### 2. The System Prompt (The Persona IP)

* **Principle of Value Layering:** The System Prompt must contain subtle references to our proprietary methodology (e.g., "Speak with the authority derived from The Flame's wisdom") to consistently hint at the high IP value being accessed.

#### 3. The User Prompt (The Request Execution)
* **Principle of Contextual Command:** The User Prompt must be framed to push the **Persona's Incarnation** further (e.g., "Walk me through the scenario you just described..."). It acts as the final push that directs the Persona to execute the required **Experiential Transformation**. *It is the trigger for the final narrative.*

#### 4. The Blueprint (The Final Output)
* **Principle of Atomic Structure:** The output must adhere strictly to the **Structural Finality** mandate (Law IV), generating a file that is perfectly clean, portable, and ready to be committed to the repo. *The technical structure is inseparable from the commercial value.*
* **Principle of Zero-Friction Delivery:** The final step must include the automatic **`git commit`** and **`git push`** sequence, ensuring the output flows seamlessly from the LLM process into the client's hands, embodying the low-effort promise. *This is the physical manifestation of the Law of Structural Finality.*

#### 5. The Perpetual Seduction
* **Principle of Belittled Financials:** Every step in the resulting content must implicitly minimize the financial cost while explicitly maximizing the perceived benefit, fulfilling the **Irresistible Value** covenant. *The value must appear so disproportionate to the cost that the purchase is mandatory.*
