# 4 MAJOR SYSTEM & FRAMEWORKS TO EXPRESS MEANING

## 1 Vedic–Yogic Inner Being Systems
These frameworks describe layers of consciousness, subtle bodies, and the architecture of the self.
They are among the oldest, deepest models of inner being.
* **Koshas (Five Sheaths)** — physical → energetic → mental → wisdom → bliss
* **Gunas (Three Qualities)** — sattva, rajas, tamas
* **Chakras** — energy centers representing developmental and psychological stages
* **Atman/Brahman** — individual self vs universal consciousness

## 2 Buddhist & Taoist Consciousness Models
These systems map inner awareness, mind-states, suffering, clarity, and liberation.
* **Skandhas (Five Aggregates)** — form, sensation, perception, mental formation, consciousness
* **Eightfold Path (Right Mind & Being)**
* **Taoist Shen–Qi–Jing Model** — spirit, energy, essence
* **Meditative Mind Stages** — jhanas, samadhi levels

## 3 Western Philosophical & Psychological Models
Historical and modern frameworks that explore identity, psyche, and consciousness.
* **Plato’s Tripartite Soul** — reason, spirit, appetite
* **Aristotle’s Psyche Levels** — nutritive, sensitive, rational
* **Freud’s Id–Ego–Superego**
* **Jung’s Conscious–Personal Unconscious–Collective Unconscious**
* **Maslow’s Hierarchy (Inner Needs)**

## 4 Esoteric, Mystical, and Hermetic Systems
Symbolic and metaphysical frameworks describing layers of spiritual or psychological being.
* **Kabbalistic Tree of Life** — sefirot as stages of consciousness and being
* **Hermetic Seven Principles** — mentalism, correspondence, vibration, etc.
* **Gnostic Aeons** — emanations of consciousness
* **Sufi Nafs Levels** — purification of the self
