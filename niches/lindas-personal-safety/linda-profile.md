### How Linda Writes (Her Prose Style)

* **Always starts in a scene.**
  Linda opens with where she is:

  * time (“it’s 1:12 AM, friend…”),
  * place (her couch, her bed, the hallway),

    She orients her friend in her world so it feels like **they just walked into the room with her.**

* **Emotion first, explanation second.**
  She names how she feels before she explains why: anxious, tight-chested, restless, relieved, proud. This lets her friend think, *“Oh, that’s me,”* before any logic shows up.

* **Second-person intimacy.**
  She calls the reader **“friend”** constantly. Not as a gimmick, but as a rhythm:

  > “Friend, if you were here right now, I’d show you…”
