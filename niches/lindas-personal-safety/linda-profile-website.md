## 4. The Flagship Concept: “Sleep-Tight Stack”

This is your **hero offer + hero narrative**. It should sit near the center of the site.

**What it is:**

> A 3–5 piece, renter-safe, repeatable **nighttime ritual** that tells her nervous system:
> *“We’ve done what we can. It’s okay to sleep now.”*

**The 5 Layers (this doubles as a product architecture):**

1. **Door Layer – Keep Them Out**

   * Portable door lock or bar (no drill).
   * Emotion: *“Even if someone tries the handle, I’m not helpless.”*

2. **Alert Layer – If Something Happens, I’ll Know**

   * Door wedge alarm / simple window sensor.
   * Emotion: *“I don’t have to stay half-awake listening. The alarm will.”*

3. **Awareness Layer – See, Don’t Imagine**

   * Peephole cam or indoor cam aimed at the door.
   * Emotion: *“Late-night knocks become boring reality, not horror-movie fanfic.”*

4. **Nervous System Layer – Let the Body Unclench**

   * White noise machine / sound app.
   * Emotion: *“Random creaks fade into a safe, predictable hum.”*

5. **Ritual Layer – The Sleep Switch**

   * A nightly, 60-second “lockdown routine”:

     * bar in place
     * alarm armed
     * cam checked
     * white noise on
   * Emotion: *“I did my part. Now I get to rest.”*

This stack should have:

* Its own **landing page** (`/sleep-tight-stack/`)
* Its own **story article** (Old Linda vs New Linda nights).
* Its own **kit version** (affiliate bundle).
