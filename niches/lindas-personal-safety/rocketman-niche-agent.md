#### 1. Define the Hero (Persona) in One Sentence

**Prompt:**

* Capture **emotion + environment**:

  * What sucks?
  * What’s unstable?
  * What’s quietly breaking them down?

---

#### 3. Identify the Deep Want vs. Surface Need

**Prompt:**
“What do they say they need? What do they actually *want* underneath?”

* Surface need = product-ish (better lock, supplement, planner).
* Deep want = intention (peace, respect, control, belonging, beauty, freedom).
* You will market **the want**, not the object.

---

#### 4. Choose the Inciting Incident (Trigger Moment)

**Prompt:**
“What single moment makes them finally say: ‘I can’t keep doing this’?”

* Make it concrete:

  * A noise, a bill, a comment, a photo, a diagnosis, a breakup, a failure.

**Prompt:**

  * Cheap fixes
  * Random hacks
  * Advice from people who don’t get it

#### 6. Reveal the Limiting Story in Their Head

**Prompt:**
“What is the lie or limiting belief that keeps them stuck?”

* Examples:

  * “I’m overreacting.”
  * “This is just how it is.”
  * “I don’t deserve to spend money on myself.”
* This is the **mental enemy**, more than the external problem.

---

#### 7. Introduce the Guide (New Perspective)

**Prompt:**
“Who shows up as the older/wiser version of them?”

* Often: your persona’s **Future Self** (e.g. New Linda), or a trusted guide/brand voice.
* Voice says: “Friend, I’ve been exactly where you are.”

---

**Prompt:**
“What is the first *actionable tool* they are given?”

* It can be:

  * A mindset shift
  * A tiny habit
  * A simple product
* Important: it’s framed as **a step toward their deep want**, not “here, buy this.”

---

**Prompt:**
“What small but real change do they feel after using this tool once?”

  * Sleeps 1 hour deeper

  * Anxiety drops a few notches
* This proves: “Change is possible for me.”

**Prompt:**

“What does life feel like now—emotionally and practically?”

* Make it **specific and grounded**, never perfect or invincible.

* They:

## 7. Techniques to Think “Flywheel” Instead of “Single Project”

Use these as mental rules:

1. **Never create a page in isolation; always define what feeds it and what it feeds.**
2. **Every story should link to at least one money page and one helper page.**
3. **Every money page should have at least 2–3 upstream feeder pages.**
4. **Every niche gets at least one persona, one hero journey, and one kit.**
5. **When something works (page, hook, product), standardize it and clone the pattern elsewhere.**
6. **Add metadata to every piece from day one (front matter + Rocketman fields).**
7. **Check your loop monthly: what’s spinning fastest, what’s stuck, where is energy leaking?**
8. **Treat cash as feedback, not just a reward: money shows where the flywheel is strongest.**
9. **Think in seasons: each season, pick one part of the flywheel to strengthen (traffic, content, conversions).**
10. **Never stop with “this page is done”—always ask, “what next page does this naturally suggest?”**
