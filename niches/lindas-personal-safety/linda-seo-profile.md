## The "Fortress" Layer (Physical Security)
Url Slug: physical-security
Goal: Immediate anxiety reduction.
- The Portable Door Lock (Addalock): Solves the "Master Key" fear instantly by mechanically jamming the door shut from the inside.
- The Security Bar: Secures sliding patio doors against the "Fishbowl" fear without needing screws or drilling.
- Privacy Window Film: Stops people from looking in without blocking the sun. Lets the light in, keeps the eyes out.

### Products for the Fortress
- **Portable Door Locks:** (e.g., Addalock, Rishon Enterprises)
- **Adjustable Security Bars:** (e.g., Master Lock Door Security Bar, Brinks)
- **Wedge Door Stop Alarms:** (e.g., SABRE, GE Personal Security)
- **Sliding Window Clamps/Limiters:** (e.g., Burglabar, Lion Lock)
- **Privacy Window Film:** (e.g., Rabbitgoo, Velimax Static Cling)
- **Heavy Duty Floor Door Jammers:** (e.g., Zuobola, SecuraDoor)

### AI Instructions for The Fortress Layer
**Role:** The Practical Engineer / The Big Sister
**Tone:** Physical, tactile, decisive.
**Key Narrative Arc:** Move the reader from "The Master Key Anxiety" (Old Linda feeling exposed) to "Mechanical Certainty" (New Linda hearing the lock click).
**Drafting Rules:**
1.  **Focus on Physics, Not Fear:** Don't describe the intruder; describe the *strength of the steel*. Use words like "jam," "wedge," "force distribution," and "mechanical override."
2.  **The Sensory Detail:** Describe the specific sound the device makes when installed. The "clunk" of the bar, the "click" of the red handle.
3.  **The Specific Enemy:** Address the "Landlord with a Key" explicitly. Validate that it is okay to physically block them out while you are inside.
4.  **Sample Prompt:** "Describe the moment Old Linda used to stare at the deadbolt wondering if it was enough, then describe the physical relief New Linda feels watching the Addalock jam into the strike plate."
