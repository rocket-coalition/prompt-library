**Rationale:**
Differentiation accelerates system-wide domain coverage and prevents content redundancy.

**Definition:**

**Requirements:**

* Each Node MUST declare one or more `preferred_routes` specifying outbound targets.
* Internal links MUST reflect intent progression, not arbitrary navigation.
* Routing MUST follow hierarchical structure: Node → Cluster → Pillar → Flywheel.

**Rationale:**
Directed flow creates predictable user journeys and reinforces structural authority.

**Rationale:**
Pillars act as system anchors, maintaining coherence and subject-matter integrity.

Mandatory metadata fields complete.

### **2.3 Linked**

Upward, lateral, and downward links present.

### **2.4 Reinforced**

Receives inbound links from other Nodes.

### **2.5 Mature**

Shows active participation in Cluster cohesion.

### **2.6 Legacy**

Eligible for review or refactor based on content age or duplication.

### **2.7 Retired**

Archived but still link-resolvable.

Nodes SHOULD NOT skip states.

Structured list of:

* upward links

* downward links
