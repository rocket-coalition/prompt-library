yamlpattern_name: "First-Person Immersion"
symbol: "◉"
category: "Embodiment Patterns"

intent: |
  Shift from external description to internal experience.
  The reader/output becomes the subject, thinking their thoughts,
  feeling their feelings, making their decisions in real-time.

structure:
  - Establish the subject/character
  - Remove narrative distance
  - Use present tense, internal sensation
  - Show thought process, not conclusions

intended_outcome: |
  Reader experiences the scenario from inside the consciousness
  of the subject. Creates empathy, understanding, and visceral
  connection impossible through external description.

when_to_apply:
  - Teaching complex decision-making processes
  - Building empathy for stakeholders
  - Creating immersive documentation
  - Character development in narratives
  - User journey mapping

example:
  user_intention: "Explain how a customer feels using our checkout"
