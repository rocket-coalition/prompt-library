## 5 DEFINITIONS OF COGNEME
1). Simplest: A cogneme is a reusable thinking instruction for AI.
2). Simple: A cogneme is a reusable unit of cognitive instruction that steers how an AI thinks.
3). Medium: A cogneme is a reusable unit of cognitive instruction that modifies how an AI processes information and generates responses.
4). Detailed: A cogneme is an atomic, reusable unit of semantic instruction that modifies an AI's cognitive behavior, steering how it analyzes, reasons about, or responds to information.
5). Most Complete: A cogneme is the smallest compositional unit of cognitive instruction—a reusable semantic component that, when introduced into an AI's context, predictably modifies its thinking patterns, analytical approach, or response generation in a specific, intended way.

## WHAT MAKES A COGNEME A COGNEME
These seven properties define the essence of what makes something a cogneme rather than just a prompt fragment or instruction.
**Essential Properties:**
- **Atomic** - Cannot be broken down further without losing meaning
- **Reusable** - Works across different contexts and tasks
- **Modifies Thinking** - Changes HOW the AI processes, not WHAT it knows
- **Self-Contained** - Complete instruction that needs no external dependencies
- **Composable** - Combines with other cognemes to create complex behaviors
- **Intent-Preserving** - Its purpose remains clear through all uses
- **Effect-Deterministic** - Produces predictable cognitive modification

## THE 4 ESSENTIAL QUESTIONS FOR ANY COGNEME

1. **Is it atomic?**
   Can you break it down further without losing its meaning?

2. **Does it change thinking?**
   Will it modify HOW the AI processes information (not just add facts)?

3. **Can it stand alone?**
   Does it work without requiring other cognemes to make sense?

4. **Can it compose?**
   Will it combine naturally with other cognemes without conflict?

If any answer is NO, it's not a proper cogneme.
