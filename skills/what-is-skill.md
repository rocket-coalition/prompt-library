## Skill

- Skills are composable
- Skills are the unit of packaging and sharing
- Skills are semantic and self discoverable
- Skills can optionally contain meta-data / frontmatter

The modular thinking from component-oriented development translates beautifully to prompts - you can have:

Composable prompt fragments (like mixins or interfaces)
Context injection patterns (dependency injection but for AI context)
